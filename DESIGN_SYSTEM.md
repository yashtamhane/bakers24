# Design System — Approved Visual Direction

## Non-negotiable art direction
Reproduce the **feel and layout hierarchy** of `references/approved-design.png`: deep chocolate-black and black-cherry atmosphere; warm amber light; cinematic, richly textured cake photography; restrained champagne highlights; elegant editorial serif headings; clean highly legible body text; soft ivory interludes on desktop; gentle organic transition curves. It must feel indulgent and alive without visual clutter.

## Working tokens (approximate, to tune against reference)
- Background dark espresso `#1A100E`; deep cocoa `#301B18`; black cherry `#511E29`.
- Warm ivory `#F7E8DA`; pale blush `#E8C4B3`; champagne `#D5AD76`; CTA wine `#8C203A`.
- These are **starting points**, not exact sampled reference colors. Audit contrast on every surface.
- Display font: licensed/free high-contrast editorial serif (e.g., Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display); interface font: legible sans (e.g., Inter). Use no more than two font families. Avoid script headlines, crown icons, gratuitous sparkles and fake metallic gradients.
- Headlines: one clear message per viewport. Paragraphs short. No stacked competing badges, claims, floating marketing slogans or excess decorative labels.
- Cards: editorial horizontal layouts on mobile menu, photography on left and name/price on right; rich solid/translucent backing with **guaranteed** text contrast; minimal borders; generous spacing; no default white card grid.
- Buttons: unmistakable filled primary CTA, readable labels, minimum 44×44px tap targets; persistent back/navigation paths.
- Motion: subtle parallax or fade/slide only where performant, 150–400ms for UI transitions; honor reduced-motion. Avoid autoplay sound, distracting petals or excessive scrolling effects.

## Mobile fidelity
- Full-bleed atmospheric home hero with logo, clear single headline, one sentence and `Explore Cakes` CTA visible without hunting.
- Menu has cocoa-toned background and a collection-specific photographic header. Search and filters remain simple and functional; list cards are readable and tappable.
- Mobile ordering is linear, forgiving, and never depends on hover or tiny controls.

## Desktop fidelity
- Split editorial hero: copy on left, dominant chocolate cake photography on right; restrained navigation; curved transition to light collection gallery.
- Do not copy every ornamental overlay from mockup; remove visual noise.

## Accessibility and performance
WCAG AA contrast for actionable text, visible focus, semantic navigation, descriptive alt text, keyboard accessibility, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, responsive image sizes, loading states, and no critical information encoded solely by color. Optimize imagery; no giant uncompressed background on mobile.

## Photography integrity
Conceptual editorial imagery may be used in the proposal with a demo label. Real portfolio photos must depict actual Baker’s 24 work; crop/correct but do not invent decorations and present them as completed orders. Replace imagery and secure usage rights before production.
